# MotorPulse AI: 20-second leadership video prompt

Create a polished, credible 20-second introduction to MotorPulse AI, an edge-first predictive-maintenance proof of concept for industrial motors. Tell a concise story from an unplanned interruption to earlier, technician-led review and planned maintenance.

## 20-second storyboard

### 00:00-00:04 | The problem
- Show a motor vibrating, a production line pausing, and an operator contacting maintenance.
- Keep the scene safe and label it `DRAMATIZATION`.
- Voice-over: “A motor changes. Production can stop without warning.”

### 00:04-00:10 | The solution
- Show a concept sensor-to-edge workflow and a signal changing from its healthy baseline.
- A technician reviews a simple alert; label the screen `CONCEPT UI — DEMO DATA`.
- On-screen text: `MotorPulse AI` / `SENSE → DETECT → PLAN`
- Voice-over: “MotorPulse flags changes from a motor’s healthy baseline.”

### 00:10-00:20 | Edge AI and prediction
- Use the two closing clips detailed at the end of this prompt.
- End on the MotorPulse AI name and `SENSE → DETECT → PLAN`.

## Production notes

- Use realistic factory visuals, restrained sound design, legible post-production text, and accurate captions.
- Keep the tone urgent but composed, then shift to calm and optimistic.
- The dashboard uses seeded or simulated data. Present all interface and workflow visuals as a concept, not a connected deployment.
- Do not imply a validated fault diagnosis, remaining-life estimate, automated motor control, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Use a warm, confident narrator and ensure the complete voice-over fits naturally within 20 seconds.

## Closing clips to add

### 00:10-00:15 | Place the edge AI box
- Show a technician positioning a compact,small  sealed edge AI box On top of a stationary motor. Keep the installation safe; show no contact with moving parts or exposed live wiring.
- On-screen text: `EDGE AI On THE MOTOR` / `CONCEPT`
- Voice-over: “A local edge AI box captures and monitors the vibration of the motor.”

### 00:15-00:20 | Mention prediction
- Show an illustrative trend extending forward from a healthy baseline, then a technician reviewing it and planning an inspection.
- On-screen text: `PREDICTION — CONCEPT` / `PROTOTYPE TODAY: ANOMALY FLAGS`
- Voice-over: “Prediction is the vision; today, the prototype flags anomalies.”
- Make clear the forecast is conceptual: the current prototype flags anomalies and does not provide a validated prediction.
